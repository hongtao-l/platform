# SeemorCam Introduction

SeemorCam is a smart pet care application that combines camera management with automatic feeding.

## Detailed Introduction

Link your cameras to your account and you can open a live view or replay recordings from anywhere, any time. Whenever the device detects pet activity or something unusual, the app sends you an instant alert so you never miss a moment. Beyond monitoring, SeemorCam includes a smart feeder for one-tap dispensing, scheduled feedings, and food-level tracking, all in a single place for managing multiple devices — giving you peace of mind about your pet's daily care.
