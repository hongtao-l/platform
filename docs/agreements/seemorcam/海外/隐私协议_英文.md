# SeemorCam Privacy Policy

> Version: 1.0
> Published / Updated: August 26, 2026
> Effective Date: August 26, 2026

**JERDERFO LIMITED** ("JERDERFO," "we," "us," or "our") develops and operates SeemorCam. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain terms, what personal data we gather from you, why we gather it, where it is stored, and the control you retain over it whenever you use SeemorCam and any related services.

SeemorCam is an application built for pet care. It lets you connect cameras to your account, view live or recorded footage from anywhere, and get notified when your pet is active or something looks out of the ordinary. A companion smart feeder adds on-demand dispensing, scheduled feedings, and food-level monitoring, so your pet's routine stays on track even while you are away.

## 1. Information We Collect

The sections below describe the categories of data we collect and the features they support.

### 1.1 Account Information

**Registration.** To open an account, you must supply at least a mobile phone number or an email address. We confirm that number or address by sending a one-time SMS code or a verification email. These details are mandatory for sign-up; the account cannot be created without them.

Anything else you add to your profile — such as extra account details or a linked third-party account — is optional. Skipping it will not stop the registration feature from working.

**Login.** Signing in requires the same phone number or email you registered with, together with a verification code or password.

### 1.2 Device and Video Data

**Device binding.** When you add a camera, you submit its serial number (SN) so we can verify and bind it. We also record whether the device is online, offline, or sleeping, so its current state can be shown to you in real time.

**Live view and playback.** With your permission, the video stream is transmitted over the network when you watch live footage or replay recordings. Live video is not retained on our servers for any extended period; recordings are kept in encrypted form either on the device itself or in cloud storage you have purchased.

### 1.3 Smart Feeder Data

When you use the feeder, we store your scheduled feeding plans, manual dispensing actions, and feeding speed settings so that instructions can be carried out and your feeding history and remaining food level can be displayed. The food-level figure comes from the device's sensor and should be treated as an estimate.

### 1.4 Notifications and Clipboard

**Notifications.** When the device detects events such as motion or pet activity, we send you a push notification. You can adjust or switch off notifications from within the App.

**Clipboard.** During login, registration, or device sharing, we may read a defined-format sharing code or device invitation code from your clipboard to auto-fill a field or open the right screen. Nothing read this way is uploaded to our servers.

### 1.5 Device Permissions and Automatically Collected Data

To keep the App stable and secure, and to enable certain features, we may request system permissions and automatically gather device-related data, including device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Android ID), screen resolution, network hardware address (MAC address), IP address, installed application list, and logs. The table below summarizes this:

| Scenario | Purpose | Method | Data Fields |
|----------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Device integration | Support core functions and device compatibility | Automatic | Client device info (model/OS version) |
| Security & risk control | Detect abnormal logins, prevent account theft | Automatic | Device MAC address, installed app list |
| Fault analysis | Diagnose crashes, improve stability | Automatic | Log information |
| Camera viewing | Live video, capturing screenshots | User-authorized | Camera permission (QR-code scanning) |
| Two-way audio | Voice intercom with the device | User-authorized | Microphone permission |
| Saving media | Save screenshots or recordings locally | User-authorized | Photo library / storage permission |
| Push notifications | Receive device alerts | User-authorized | Notification permission |
| Device connection | Wi-Fi or Bluetooth provisioning | User-authorized | Location (Android Wi-Fi scanning), Bluetooth permission |
| Analytics | Measure feature usage, refine product flows | Automatic | Interaction tracking (user behavior records) |
| Network services | Data loading and server communication | Automatic | Network status (Wi-Fi / cellular) |
| Device management | Verify device legitimacy, bind warranty service | Automatic | Device serial number (SN) |

> On the MAC address: it is used only for device recognition, security checks, and anonymized statistics, and it is hash-encrypted rather than tied directly to your identity.
>
> On the installed application list: it is used only to check the operating environment, measure ad performance, and run compatibility analysis; we collect the minimum list required for functionality and do not continuously track your usage.

Most operating systems (Android, iOS, and others) let you manage permissions in your system settings; revoking a permission may disable part of the feature that depends on it. Device information and logs, taken on their own, cannot identify a specific natural person. If we ever combine such non-personal information with other data in a way that identifies you, or merge it with personal information, we will treat the combined data as personal information for as long as it remains combined. Except where you have authorized us or the law requires otherwise, we anonymize or de-identify this information.
