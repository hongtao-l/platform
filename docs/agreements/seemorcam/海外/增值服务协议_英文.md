## 1. Description of SeemorCam Cloud Services

**1.1** A cloud service is bound simultaneously to the user account and to the device. In other words, the entitlement is valid only for the combination of "the account used at purchase + the device used at purchase." As soon as a device is unbound and rebound to a different SeemorCam account, the original entitlement stops applying to that device, and the new account must place a separate order before it can use the service.

**1.2** At present, cloud services do not offer migration of entitlements between devices, nor do they support suspension mid-term.

**1.3** Cloud services obtained through non-standard channels (for example, promotional gifts, discounted redemption, or conversion from a trial) are likewise not transferable between devices.

**1.4** Cloud storage places certain demands on the network. We recommend maintaining an uplink bandwidth of no less than 2 Mbps per device. Please confirm your network conditions before ordering, so that recording upload and playback are not interrupted or degraded.
