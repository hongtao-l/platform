# oksee Introduction

oksee is a companion app from Zhongshan Mingyang Liangguang Lighting Co., Ltd. that gathers every smart camera you own into a single phone screen.

## Detailed Introduction

Adding a new camera to oksee takes only a few steps: join it to your home network and bind it to your account, and it appears in your device list. From there you can open a live view at any hour, wherever you happen to be, and scroll back through stored footage on a timeline. When the camera spots a person or movement, a notification reaches your phone straight away, so you know what is happening at the door, in the yard, or at the warehouse without having to look. Night-time imaging, snapshot capture, and device sharing round out everyday use—family members can watch the same camera without exchanging passwords—and multiple cameras sit side by side in one list, ready to switch between.
