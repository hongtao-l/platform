## 1. Description of oksee Cloud Services

**1.1** Cloud Services rest on two pillars: the account and the device. An entitlement you purchase operates only on the specific device held under the account that placed the order. Should that device be unbound and rebound to a different oksee account, the entitlement ceases to operate on it, and the new account must place its own order before the service can be used again.

**1.2** Entitlements cannot at present be moved from one device to another, and service cannot be paused.

**1.4** For cloud storage, a single device should have uplink bandwidth of no less than 2 Mbps. Please confirm before ordering that your network can sustain that rate, so that upload and playback are not impaired.

### 2.2 Automatic Renewal

#### 2.2.1 What the Feature Does

**2.2.1.1** Automatic renewal is a feature through which, following the settings you choose, fees are deducted automatically from a third-party payment account you designate.

**2.2.1.2** Please read and fully understand every clause in this Section, especially those that exclude or limit liability. Minors should read this Agreement together with a legal guardian and confirm that they may bind another payment account.

**2.2.1.3** Clicking to activate automatic renewal and then using it means you accept this Section. If you object to any part of it, choose not to activate the feature.

#### 2.2.2 What It Covers

**2.2.2.1** So that you can keep the exclusive benefits of the Cloud Services without interruption, we offer automatic renewal. Once you expressly agree to activate it, you authorize us, whenever the agreed renewal conditions are met, to deduct from the payment account you have bound the amount needed to buy the paid service again.

**2.2.2.2** The feature applies to plan-based services offered by Mingyang Liangguang that are renewed periodically, including the "Cloud Services."

#### 2.2.3 Turning It On

**2.2.3.1** Once automatic renewal is on, your oksee account is bound to the third-party payment account you designate.

**2.2.3.2** Before your current plan term expires, we deduct the fee for the same plan type, alert you to the renewal by SMS or another channel, and carry forward the corresponding service content, conditions, and term.

**2.2.3.3** You must ensure that payment can be taken successfully from that account. Where renewal fails—because the balance available in the account is insufficient, for example—we may interrupt or terminate the corresponding Services provided to you.

#### 2.2.4 When the Deduction Happens

24 hours before each plan term expires, the system completes the deduction and extends the term automatically, according to the plan type you bought.

#### 2.2.5 Turning It Off

Once automatic renewal is off, no renewal takes place and no automatic deduction is initiated for the next plan cycle. Fees already paid for the current plan cycle are not refundable.

#### 2.2.6 Changes to This Agreement

**2.2.6.1** Given how the internet evolves, changes in laws, regulations, and normative documents, and the needs of our business, we may amend or vary the clauses of this Agreement. Where its content changes, we will notify you through one or more of the following, as circumstances warrant: system messages, mobile push notifications, SMS, or email.

**2.2.6.2** If you do not agree with an amendment we make, you may turn automatic renewal off. Continuing to use the Services means you accept the amendment.

#### 2.2.7 Other Matters

**2.2.7.1** Where fees cannot be deducted because of force majeure or another cause outside our control, we will deal with the consequences appropriately.

**2.2.7.2** Questions about this Section may be sent to customer service at dev@spmled.com.cn.
