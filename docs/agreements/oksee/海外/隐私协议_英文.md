# oksee Privacy Policy

> Version: 1.0
> Published / Updated: October 27, 2026
> Effective Date: October 27, 2026

**Zhongshan Mingyang Liangguang Lighting Co., Ltd.** ("Mingyang Liangguang," "we," "us," or "our") builds and operates oksee, a companion application for smart cameras. oksee lets you bring a camera onto your Wi-Fi network, bind it to your account, watch live footage from anywhere, review saved recordings on a timeline, receive alerts the moment the camera notices movement or a person, and keep every device you own organized in one place.

This Policy explains what personal information oksee handles, why we need it, who else may see it, and what you can do about it. We have set out the essentials in plain language and flagged the points that deserve a closer look. Please give it a careful read before you create an account. Should anything here leave you uncertain, write to us at the address at the end of this document and we will explain. Choosing to register, or continuing to use oksee after this Policy takes effect, tells us that you have read and accepted what follows.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

To open an oksee account you supply either a mobile number or an email address. We send a one-time code by SMS, or a verification link by email, to establish that the applicant controls that identifier. Without it, no account can be created. Once your account exists, you may add a display name or avatar, or sign in through a third-party identity provider; neither step is required, and skipping them will not prevent you from using the core features. Each time you sign in, we check the credentials you present—your registered number or address together with the code or password you enter.

### 1.2 Device and Video Data

Binding a camera requires the device serial number (SN) so that we can confirm the unit is genuine and record the ownership link. We also read the camera's operating state (online, offline, or in sleep mode) in order to display it correctly in your device list.

Live streaming and playback involve the transmission of video data over the network, which happens only after you grant the relevant permission. Live footage is not retained on our servers for any extended period. Recordings are kept in encrypted form either on the camera's local storage or within cloud storage that you have purchased separately.

### 1.3 Alerts, Notifications, and Clipboard Access

When a camera registers a human shape, movement, or sound, we deliver a push notification to your device. Notification categories and quiet hours can be adjusted, or the feature switched off entirely, from the app settings.

If you share a camera with a family member, we record the identifier of the account receiving the share so that the authorization can be established. A recipient obtains only the level of access you grant and does not gain access to your account details.

In the sign-in, registration, and device-invitation flows, the app may read a share code or invitation code from your clipboard in order to prefill a field or open the right screen. This reading occurs on your own device; the clipboard contents are never transmitted to our servers.

### 1.4 Permissions and Automatically Collected Data

Certain features depend on permissions granted through your operating system. In addition, we automatically collect limited technical data that keeps the service running and secure:

**Device details.** Device model, operating system version, a unique device identifier such as the Android ID, screen resolution, the network hardware address (MAC address), and IP address. These support normal operation, account risk assessment, and fault diagnosis.

> **A note on the MAC address**
> Why we need it: to recognize devices, to detect account theft and unusual sign-ins, and—once stripped of any link to you—to compile aggregate usage figures.
> How we handle it: the MAC address is hashed as soon as it is collected and is never matched directly against your identity.

**Installed application list.** In some system environments we read the package names of apps installed on your device.
> Why we need it: to verify the runtime environment, measure the reach of promotions, refine advertising content, and support compatibility and security analysis.
> How we handle it: we collect only the entries relevant to app functionality and do not track how you use your applications over time.

**Permissions in context**

Android, iOS, and other platforms all provide permission controls, and you may revoke any grant at any time from system settings—though the corresponding feature will then stop working. A device model or a log entry, taken alone, does not point to any particular individual. Where such data must be combined with other information to identify a person, or used alongside personal information, we treat it as personal information for as long as that combination lasts and apply de-identification or anonymization as the law requires.

To deliver oksee end to end, personal information relating to you may move between the operating entities of the various applications within the Mingyang Liangguang group. Unless stated otherwise, each of those entities is an affiliate of Mingyang Liangguang. Any such movement is tied to a specific, defined, and lawful purpose, and covers only the information the service actually requires.

Beyond that, oksee does not presently disclose personal information in any other form, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

We use the information described above to operate and maintain the service, to verify identity, to answer support requests, to detect and prevent fraud, to diagnose faults, to understand how features are used so that we can improve them, and to meet our legal obligations. Where we rely on your consent—for example, for camera, microphone, or notification access—you may withdraw it at any time through your device settings, although the related feature will then be unavailable.

## 4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as the purposes described in this Policy require, unless a longer period is mandated by law. When that period ends, the information is deleted or anonymized.

Personal information collected and generated in mainland China is stored in mainland China. Information relating to users outside mainland China may be processed where our service providers operate, as described in Section 5.

We protect information through encryption in transit (SSL/TLS), access controls, data classification, and periodic security audits, together with organizational safeguards covering staff access and incident response. No system is entirely immune to attack; if a security incident affecting your information occurs, we will notify you as required by law, explaining what happened, what the likely impact is, and what we have done or intend to do about it.

Because oksee is offered in more than one region, your personal information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in a country other than the one in which you live. Where we make such a transfer, we put appropriate safeguards in place in line with applicable data protection law.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to your jurisdiction and to certain legal exceptions, you may exercise the following rights:

- **Access** — ask whether we hold information about you and obtain a copy of it.
- **Rectification** — have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected.
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your information, including by closing your account.
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we process your information while a concern is being examined.
- **Portability** — receive the information you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller where technically feasible.
- **Objection** — object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests.
- **Withdrawal of consent** — withdraw any consent you have given at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
- **Complaint** — lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Many of these actions are available directly in the app under account settings. For anything else, write to dev@spmled.com.cn. We verify your identity before acting and reply within the period required by applicable law.

## 7. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on the following bases:

- **Contract** — processing needed to provide oksee to you, including account creation, device binding, streaming, and playback.
- **Consent** — processing tied to optional permissions such as camera, microphone, notifications, Bluetooth, and location during network setup. You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legitimate interests** — keeping the service secure, preventing fraud and abuse, diagnosing faults, and improving features, balanced against your rights and expectations.
- **Legal obligation** — processing required to comply with applicable law or a binding order from a competent authority.

## 8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you reside in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the following rights:

- **Right to know** — request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purposes, and the categories of recipients.
- **Right to delete** — request deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to statutory exceptions.
- **Right to correct** — request correction of inaccurate personal information.
- **Right to opt out** — we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
- **Right to limit use of sensitive personal information** — we do not use sensitive personal information beyond the purposes permitted by statute.
- **Right to non-discrimination** — we will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or provide a lower level of service because you exercised your rights.

An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with written authorization. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dev@spmled.com.cn.
