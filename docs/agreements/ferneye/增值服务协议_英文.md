## 1. Description of FernEye Cloud Services

**1.1** Cloud Services are bound jointly to the user account and the device. A Cloud Service entitlement applies only to the specific device under the account that purchased it. Once a device is unbound and rebound to a different FernEye account, the original entitlement ceases to apply to that device, and the new account must purchase the Cloud Service separately before it can be used.

**1.2** Cloud Services currently do not support entitlement transfer between devices, nor do they support suspension of service.

**1.3** Cloud Services purchased at a non-standard price (for example, obtained through promotional giveaways or discounted redemption) do not support transfer between devices.

**1.4** To use cloud storage services, a single device should have at least 2 Mbps of uplink bandwidth. Please confirm before ordering that your network environment meets this requirement, so that recording upload and playback are not affected.

#### 2.2.5 Turning Off Automatic Renewal

Once automatic renewal is turned off, no further renewal will occur and no automatic deduction will be initiated for the next plan cycle. Fees already paid for the current plan cycle are non-refundable.
