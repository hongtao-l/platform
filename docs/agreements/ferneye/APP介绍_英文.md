# FernEye Introduction

FernEye is a smart camera device management and monitoring application built for hunting and outdoor scenarios.

## Detailed Introduction

With FernEye, you can easily pair and bind your hunting cameras, remotely view live field feeds and replay recorded footage anytime, anywhere. When the device detects animals or motion, it sends instant alert notifications to help you track game movements. It also supports night vision, device sharing, and unified multi-device management, providing reliable support for your hunting and outdoor observation.
