## 1. Privacy Notice

The collection, storage, use, and security management of your personal information under the Wildeye Service are governed by this Privacy Policy. To help you clearly understand the specifics, we highlight the key points as follows:

### 1.1 How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

We collect and use your personal information only for the purposes described in this Policy:

#### 1.1.1 Core Features and Services

**(a) Account Registration**
When you register a Wildeye account, you must provide at least your mobile phone number or email address. We verify your identity by sending an SMS verification code to your phone or a verification email to your inbox. This information is necessary for the registration function; if you do not provide it, you will not be able to complete registration.

You may also choose to supplement your account with additional information or link third-party accounts as prompted by specific feature pages. Such information is not required for using the Wildeye Service, and refusing to provide it will not affect the use of the account registration function.

**(b) Account Login**
When you log in to your Wildeye account, you must provide your registration credentials, including the mobile phone number/email and verification code or password used during registration. This information is necessary for the login functionality.

**(c) Device Binding and Management**
When adding and managing Wildeye smart camera devices, you must provide the device serial number (SN) for device authentication and binding. We also collect device status information (online/offline/sleep) to display real-time device status.

**(d) Live Video and Playback**
When using live video viewing or historical playback features, we transmit video stream data over the network with your authorization. We do not store your live video footage on our servers long-term. Recording files are stored in encrypted form on the device locally or in your purchased cloud storage space.

**(e) Push Notifications**
When the device detects abnormal events (human detection, motion detection, sound detection, etc.), we notify you via push notifications. You can manage push preferences or disable notifications in the App settings.

**(f) Clipboard Function**
When using login/registration or device sharing features, we may locally read clipboard content (limited to specific-format sharing codes or device invitation codes) for quick auto-fill or navigation to the corresponding feature page. This process does not upload clipboard content to our servers.

#### 1.1.2 Device Permissions and Related Information Collection

To ensure the App operates normally and securely, and to implement specific service features, we may request operating system permissions and collect relevant device information. This includes:

**Device Basic Information**
We collect your device model, operating system version, unique device identifier (e.g., Android ID), screen resolution, network hardware address (MAC address), IP address, and other information to ensure normal service usage, security risk control, and troubleshooting.

> Regarding MAC Address:
> - Purpose: Device identification, security risk control (preventing account theft and abnormal logins), and anonymized statistical analysis (e.g., user volume statistics, advertising effectiveness measurement)
> - Processing: We hash-encrypt the MAC address and do not directly associate it with your personal identity information

**Installed Application List**
We may collect information about the application list (Package Name) installed on your device.
> - Purpose: Primarily to check the operating environment to ensure core functionality, analyze advertising effectiveness, and optimize advertising content. Also used for security and compatibility analysis to ensure stable operation of the application
> - Processing: We only collect necessary application lists related to app functionality and do not continuously monitor your app usage behavior

You can disable corresponding permissions in your device's settings, though this may affect the use of related features. Individual device information and log information alone cannot identify a specific natural person. If such non-personal information needs to be combined with other information to identify a specific natural person, or combined with personal information, then for the duration of such combined use, such non-personal information will be treated as personal information. Unless authorized by you or otherwise required by law, we will anonymize or de-identify such personal information.
